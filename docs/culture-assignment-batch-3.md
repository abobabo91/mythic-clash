- `Assyrian`
- `Maori`

## 1. Assyrian

## Final assignment

- Primary family: `Vanguard`
- Secondary leaning: `Tempest`
- Signature Character candidate: `Ashur`

## Why this is the right fit

Assyrian is one of the clearest war-state cultures in the current pool.

Its strongest signals are:

- war
- kingship
- sky
- justice
- storm-backed force

It also has wisdom and underworld notes, but those read best as secondary texture.

The strongest competitive read is:

- imperial military pressure with divine storm support

## What players should feel facing Assyrian

- heavy attack pressure
- command-level buffs
- punishing battle phases
- some storm-backed tempo swings

## What to emphasize

- war gods
- kingship
- disciplined aggression
- offensive mass-buff turns
- selective storm pressure

## What to de-emphasize

- too much graveyard identity
- too much broad wisdom/control as primary plan
- too much plague as the faction’s headline

Those can stay, but not dominate the read.

## Why Ashur is the best Signature Character

`Ashur` is the cleanest expression of:

- state power
- war
- kingship
- divine authority

`Ishtar of Nineveh` is very strong and iconic, but she pushes the culture slightly more toward a broader mythic spectrum than the clean primary read.

## Signature Character pattern

Recommended twist:

- once each turn, after you attack with multiple Figures, Ashur may convert that offensive push into a battlefield decree such as teamwide attack pressure, forced target priority, or a clean bonus strike rider

## Supporting cast shape

- 8 Vanguard cards
- 4 to 5 Tempest cards
- 2 Oracle cards
- very light Gravebound splash

## 2. Maori

## Final assignment

- Primary family: `Tidebound`
- Secondary leaning: `Wildhost`
- Signature Character candidate: `Maui`

## Why this is the right fit

Maori is broad and powerful, but it still resolves cleanly.

Its strongest current signals are:

- sea
- water
- sky
- creation
- earth
- fertility

That makes the clearest read:

- sea-led tempo and movement with strong creation/nature backup

This is better than making Maori mainly Tempest or mainly Wildhost.

## What players should feel facing Maori

- motion
- pull and flow
- tactical pressure from sea-space control
- nature-backed board resilience

## What to emphasize

- sea and water tempo
- movement and drag effects
- world-shaping creation flavor
- secondary growth and token support

## What to de-emphasize

- too much generic storm-burn as the center
- too much split identity between all major gods equally
- too much broad underworld focus

## Why Maui is the best Signature Character

This one is slightly counterintuitive, but I think `Maui` is the best choice.

Why:

- he is extremely memorable
- he naturally supports a trickier, movement-based, tactical edge
- he gives the faction one strong recognizable twist without redefining the whole culture

`Tane Mahuta` is also excellent, but he pulls Maori more toward a grounded Wildhost identity than the broad sea-tempo read suggests.

## Signature Character pattern

Recommended twist:

- once each turn, Maui may reposition, reverse, or distort one combat or stat interaction in a bounded way

That gives the culture a memorable tactical hook while the rest stays readable.

## Supporting cast shape

- 7 Tidebound cards
- 5 Wildhost cards
- 3 Tempest cards
- light Sanctuary only

## 3. Celtic Welsh

## Final assignment

- Primary family: `Oracle`
- Secondary leaning: `Vanguard`
- Signature Character candidate: `Gwydion`

## Why this is the right fit

Celtic Welsh currently has:

- magic
- fate
- sovereignty
- war
- moon
- transformation
- Annwn / Otherworld

It could become too diffuse very easily.

The clearest competitive read is:

- magical tactical control with noble combat pressure behind it

That means:

- more Oracle than broad fantasy spectacle
- more precision and manipulation than raw aggression

## What players should feel facing Celtic Welsh

- clever timing
- manipulation
- fate pressure
- selective battlefield control
- elegant, sometimes cruel tactical pivots

## What to emphasize

- magical planning
- transformation and illusion in bounded form
- fate-locking
- tactical setup into combat payoff

## What to de-emphasize

- too much resurrection as a major theme
- too much broad Tempest identity
- too much random trickster chaos

## Why Gwydion is the best Signature Character

`Gwydion` is the best faction face because he captures:

- magic
- trickery
- war
- sky-linked mythic power

`Arianrhod` is extremely strong and elegant, but feels slightly narrower and more static as a faction face.

## Signature Character pattern

Recommended twist:

- once each turn, Gwydion may create a decoy, redirect a hostile action, or turn one targeted interaction into an illusion-based misfire

This is memorable, skillful, and still answerable.

## Supporting cast shape

- 7 Oracle cards
- 5 Vanguard cards
- 2 Tidebound/Moon cards
- very light Sanctuary or Gravebound support only

## 4. Maya

## Final assignment

- Primary family: `Oracle`
- Secondary leaning: `Tempest`
- Signature Character candidate: `Itzamna`

## Why this is the right fit

Maya currently has many valid signals:

- wisdom
- sky
- wind
- storm
- maize/fertility
- death/rebirth
- serpent imagery

It is easy to misread Maya as pure Tempest because of storm presence, but I think the cleaner and stronger competitive identity is:

- wisdom-led celestial planning with forceful elemental follow-through

That makes them feel distinct from Aztec / Mexica.

## What players should feel facing Maya

- intelligent setup
- predictive card flow
- celestial tempo
- bursts of storm pressure at the right moment

## What to emphasize

- sky and wisdom
- divination
- serpent/celestial knowledge
- measured but dangerous elemental pressure

## What to de-emphasize

- too much broad Wildhost identity
- too much generic death-recursion as main plan
- too much overlap with Aztec’s sacrifice-pressure style

## Why Itzamna is the best Signature Character

`Itzamna` is the cleanest center for this faction:

- wisdom
- creation
- sky
- light

He gives Maya a smart and distinctive identity.

`Kukulkan` is iconic and should matter a lot, but he reads better as a major secondary star than the single defining faction face.

## Signature Character pattern

Recommended twist:

- once each turn, after you draw, discover, or reveal information, Itzamna may convert that knowledge into a bounded combat or spell bonus

This is perfect Oracle signature space.

## Supporting cast shape

- 7 Oracle cards
- 5 Tempest cards
- 3 Wildhost cards
- 2 Gravebound cards max

## 5. Muisca Colombian

## Final assignment

- Primary family: `Sanctuary`
- Secondary leaning: `Oracle`
- Signature Character candidate: `Bochica`

## Why this is the right fit

Muisca Colombian reads very beautifully and more cleanly than I expected.

Its strongest signals are:

- light
- water
- wisdom
- justice
- fertility
- creation

The clearest read is:

- luminous civilizing protection and wisdom

That is stronger and more distinctive than making it a generic mixed light/nature faction.

## What players should feel facing Muisca Colombian

- cleansing and protection
- wise control
- stable but growing board value
- calm, high-integrity pressure

## What to emphasize

- light and wisdom
- protection and healing
- justice
- civilization-building support
- lake / water spiritual support as a secondary tone

## What to de-emphasize

- too much trickster energy from Huitaca
- too much broad Tempest pressure
- too much split between all moon/sun/water aspects equally

## Why Bochica is the best Signature Character

`Bochica` is the clearest culture face:

- wise
- civilizing
- just
- iconic

`Chiminigagua` is strong and mythically central, but reads more like a broad creator anchor than the sharpest gameplay signpost.

## Signature Character pattern

Recommended twist:

- once each turn, after you heal, reveal, or advise, Bochica may grant a secondary wisdom-based correction effect such as cleanse, expose, or tactical stat shaping

This is very aligned with Sanctuary + Oracle.

## Supporting cast shape

- 8 Sanctuary cards
- 4 to 5 Oracle cards
- 2 Tidebound cards
- light Wildhost support only

## 6. Georgian Nart

## Final assignment

- Primary family: `Vanguard`
- Secondary leaning: `Tempest`
- Signature Character candidate: `Amirani`

## Why this is the right fit

Georgian Nart is one of the strongest natural war cultures in the set.

Its clearest signals are:

- mountain
- war
- strength
- fire
- storm
- heroic action

That makes the read very strong:

- mountain-war pressure with mythic elemental force

## What players should feel facing Georgian Nart

- aggressive hero pressure
- heavy battlefield swings
- explosive but visible finish pressure
- mountain-rooted combat identity

## What to emphasize

- heroes first
- mountain-war identity
- strike pressure
- fire and storm as force multipliers

## What to de-emphasize

- too much Oracle identity from Satanaya as the center
- too much trickster noise from Syrdon
- too much broad Wildhost spillover

Those are useful secondary notes, but not the faction face.

## Why Amirani is the best Signature Character

`Amirani` is a perfect signature candidate:

- legendary hero
- rebellious
- fiery
- mountain-rooted
- instantly memorable

He reads exactly like the kind of one-per-culture standout we want.

## Signature Character pattern

Recommended twist:

- once each turn, after Amirani survives combat or deals combat damage, his next fire-based offensive effect becomes empowered

That gives him a dramatic but still answerable identity.

## Supporting cast shape

- 8 Vanguard cards
- 4 to 5 Tempest cards
- 2 Oracle cards
- very light Trickster or Wildhost only

## What We Learned From Batch 3

### 1. Some of the strongest cultures are war cultures with different flavors

`Assyrian` and `Georgian Nart` are both clearly war-forward, but they still resolve differently:

- Assyrian feels imperial-command
- Georgian Nart feels heroic-mountain-force

That is exactly the kind of distinction we want.

### 2. Oracle cultures can still feel very different

`Celtic Welsh` and `Maya` both land in Oracle space, but:

- Welsh reads magical-illusionary-fate
- Maya reads celestial-knowledge-storm

That is a healthy difference.

### 3. Signature Characters continue to solve the flavor problem well

They let the culture keep one strong, memorable, slightly irregular face while the rest of the faction remains readable.
