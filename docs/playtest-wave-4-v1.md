# Playtest Wave 4 v1

Last updated: 2026-05-04

Wave 4 translated:

- `guarani`
- `mapuche`

These packs were validated as legal `24`-card decks and checked with the newer rollout/smoke test stack.

## Guarani

Identity read:

- `Wildhost` primary
- `Tempest` secondary
- trickster token pressure with visible storm damage follow-through

Smoke note:

- healthy into `Haitian Vodou` and `Inuit`
- competitive but not dominant into `Ancient Greek`
- still wants benchmark-tier follow-up when we revisit wave 4 under the latest bot tuning

Main gameplay feel:

- swarms early bodies
- pushes damage through token glue and burn
- is readable on board and does not hide its plan behind weird private mechanics

## Mapuche

Identity read:

- `Tempest` primary
- `Sanctuary` secondary
- serpent tempo plus sturdy guard/protection shell

Smoke note:

- strong smoke-tier results
- earlier benchmark-tier testing showed a much tougher `Ancient Greek` matchup
- should be treated as "promising but not benchmark-cleared"

Main gameplay feel:

- wins through bounce, freeze, guards, and clean storm swings
- feels different from Guarani without becoming harder to parse

## Practical meaning

Wave 4 succeeded at readability.

The remaining question for these two cultures is not "do they make sense?"

It is:

- how much of their strength survives the slower benchmark tier
- whether `Ancient Greek` is still the real gate they must pass later
