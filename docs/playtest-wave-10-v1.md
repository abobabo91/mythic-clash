# Playtest Wave 10 v1

Last updated: 2026-05-04

Wave 10 translated:

- `ancient-egyptian`
- `norse-viking`
- `roman`
- `celtic-irish`
- `maya`

All five packs:

- export cleanly
- validate as legal `24`-card lists
- passed rollout smoke tests

## Ancient Egyptian

Identity read:

- `Sanctuary` primary
- `Gravebound` secondary
- shrine-control with judgment removal, durable guards, and a sharper sun-war top end

Smoke record after deck retune:

- `1-1` vs `Ancient Greek`
- `2-0` vs `Haitian Vodou`
- `2-0` vs `Inuit`

Both-seat spot check vs `Ancient Greek`:

- `2-2`

Main gameplay feel:

- the original list was too shrine-heavy and let Greek set the pace too easily
- once the deck moved toward `Ammit`, `Sobek`, `Apophis`, `Horus`, `Ra`, and `Set`, the culture became much more credible
- still clearly defensive in identity
- no longer feels like it auto-loses to the strongest early board deck

## Norse-Viking

Identity read:

- `Vanguard` primary
- `Tempest` secondary
- rush-first combat pressure with storm clears and a sturdy heroic curve

Smoke record:

- `1-1` vs `Ancient Greek`
- `2-0` vs `Haitian Vodou`
- `2-0` vs `Inuit`

Both-seat spot check vs `Ancient Greek`:

- `1-3`

Main gameplay feel:

- still one of the cleaner successful aggressive packs
- very readable and proactive
- strong on the play, but still softer from the draw into Greek's best openings
- healthy enough to keep without extra rework right now

## Roman

Identity read:

- `Vanguard` primary
- `Oracle` secondary
- disciplined legion tempo with rush pressure, forge damage, and measured card selection

Smoke record after deck retune:

- `1-1` vs `Ancient Greek`
- `1-1` vs `Haitian Vodou`
- `1-1` vs `Inuit`

Both-seat spot check vs `Ancient Greek`:

- `2-2`

Main gameplay feel:

- the first Roman list was too guard-heavy and too polite
- the retuned list finally behaves like a legion deck instead of a midrange waiting room
- `Vulcan`, `Cacus`, `Mercury`, and `Romulus` gave it the tempo it was missing
- now reads clearly and plays like a fair but real pressure deck

## Celtic Irish

Identity read:

- `Oracle` primary
- `Trickster` secondary
- fate-reading tempo with bounce, rush pivots, and selective removal

Smoke record after deck retune:

- `0-2` vs `Ancient Greek`
- `2-0` vs `Haitian Vodou`
- `2-0` vs `Inuit`

Both-seat spot check vs `Ancient Greek`:

- `0-4`

Main gameplay feel:

- the culture is readable and fun, but it is still too soft into Greek specifically
- the second list was much healthier into the broader field than the stealth-heavy first draft
- even after the retune, the Greek matchup remains the clearest unresolved problem of the batch
- this looks like a deck-level and possibly hero-power tension, not a total pack failure

## Maya

Identity read:

- `Oracle` primary
- `Tempest` secondary
- codex-tempo control with sharper storm answers and visible pressure turns

Smoke record after deck retune:

- `1-1` vs `Ancient Greek`
- `2-0` vs `Haitian Vodou`
- `2-0` vs `Inuit`

Both-seat spot check vs `Ancient Greek`:

- `2-2`

Main gameplay feel:

- the original list was too soft and too healing-centric
- once it leaned harder into `Bolon Yokte`, `Cabrakan`, `Hunahpu`, and `Huracan`, the pack came alive
- now feels like a strong success for the oracle-tempest family
- readable, interactive, and capable of ending games with visible storm pressure

## Practical meaning

Wave 10 was a strong batch overall.

`Ancient Egyptian`, `Roman`, and `Maya` all proved that several of the earlier weak results were deck-construction problems rather than translation failures.

`Norse-Viking` remains healthy and straightforward, even if it still loses some draw-side Greek games.

`Celtic Irish` is the only wave-10 culture that still fails the Greek gate cleanly. That is important, but it is also a narrow problem:

- it wins its secondary rollout checks
- it keeps its intended identity
- it just still needs a stronger anti-board posture against the hardest benchmark

The broader signal is good:

- the rollout method is still holding on high-profile cultures
- sample deck tuning matters a lot
- `Ancient Greek` remains the best pressure gate for new translations
