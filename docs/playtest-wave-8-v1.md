# Playtest Wave 8 v1

Last updated: 2026-05-04

Wave 8 translated:

- `haida`
- `olmec`
- `zapotec`

All three packs:

- export cleanly
- validate as legal `24`-card lists
- passed rollout smoke tests

## Haida

Identity read:

- `Tidebound` primary
- `Trickster` secondary
- bounce, stealth, sea pressure, and guarded tempo stabilization

Smoke record:

- `1-1` vs `Ancient Greek`
- `2-0` vs `Haitian Vodou`
- `2-0` vs `Inuit`

Both-seat spot check vs `Ancient Greek`:

- `1-3`

Main gameplay feel:

- distinct and readable
- uses bounce and stealth without becoming obscure
- strong when it gets to sequence tempo turns from the play
- still softer on the draw into the strongest Greek curves

## Olmec

Identity read:

- `Wildhost` primary
- `Sanctuary` secondary
- earth-beast pressure with maize recovery and visible storm stabilization

Smoke record after deck retune:

- `1-1` vs `Ancient Greek`
- `1-1` vs `Haitian Vodou`
- `2-0` vs `Inuit`

Both-seat spot check vs `Ancient Greek`:

- `1-3`

Main gameplay feel:

- the pack identity was sound from the start
- the first sample deck was too polite and underused its storm stabilizers
- after swapping in `Rain Spirit` and `Night Sun`, the deck became much healthier
- still not favored into Greek from the draw, but no longer collapses immediately

## Zapotec

Identity read:

- `Tempest` primary
- `Sanctuary` secondary
- storm-control with fertility healing and stone-ancestor shelter

Smoke record:

- `1-1` vs `Ancient Greek`
- `2-0` vs `Haitian Vodou`
- `2-0` vs `Inuit`

Both-seat spot check vs `Ancient Greek`:

- `1-3`

Main gameplay feel:

- very easy to understand on first read
- good use of storm control and healing without hidden blowouts
- strong enough to compete on the play
- still seat-sensitive into Greek tempo, like several other rollout packs

## Practical meaning

Wave 8 broadened the translated set in a healthy way.

`Haida` and `Zapotec` both landed quickly as readable, matchup-healthy cultures.

`Olmec` was another useful reminder that deck construction matters as much as pack translation. The culture did not need strange new mechanics. It needed a better sample list.
