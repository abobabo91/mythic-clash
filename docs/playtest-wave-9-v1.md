## Inca-Andean

Identity read:

- `Sanctuary` primary
- `Tempest` secondary
- mountain-sun shelter with storm interaction and steady wisdom smoothing

Smoke record after deck retune:

- `1-1` vs `Ancient Greek`
- `2-0` vs `Haitian Vodou`
- `2-0` vs `Inuit`

Both-seat spot check vs `Ancient Greek`:

- `1-3`

Main gameplay feel:

- the original sample deck was too passive
- once the list leaned harder into `Catequil`, `Ayar Auca`, `Ayar Cachi`, and `Illapa`, the culture became much healthier
- still not favored into Greek from the draw
- now clearly rollout-healthy instead of obviously underbuilt

## Practical meaning

Wave 9 was a useful stress batch.

`Ancient Chinese` landed immediately as one of the stronger clean-fit cultures.

`Inca-Andean` confirmed again that some weak results come from sample-deck construction rather than from the pack translation itself.

The broader pattern is still clear:

- most rollout cultures can compete on the play
- `Ancient Greek` remains the harshest both-seat benchmark
- we should keep treating Greek as the main pressure gate while we continue scaling the roster
