# Playtest Wave 6 v1

Last updated: 2026-05-04

Wave 6 translated:

- `apache`
- `navajo-dine`

Both packs:

- export cleanly
- validate as legal `24`-card lists
- passed rollout smoke tests

## Apache

Identity read:

- `Vanguard` primary
- `Sanctuary` secondary
- heroic combat pressure with mountain guards and healing backup

Smoke record:

- `0-2` vs `Ancient Greek`
- `2-0` vs `Haitian Vodou`
- `2-0` vs `Inuit`

Main gameplay feel:

- clear rush/combat plan
- readable stabilization tools
- healthy into slower value and tempo decks
- still soft into the strongest early Greek pressure lines

This is a healthy rollout pack, but not a benchmark-cleared one.

## Navajo-Dine

Identity read:

- `Sanctuary` primary
- `Oracle` secondary
- healing ritual control with selection and clean monster-answer tools

Smoke record:

- `0-2` vs `Ancient Greek`
- `1-1` vs `Haitian Vodou`
- `2-0` vs `Inuit`

Main gameplay feel:

- calm and readable
- good card smoothing
- visible comeback tools
- lighter closing pressure than some other translated packs

This pack is stable enough to keep, but it is closer to the middle of the field than to the top.

## Practical meaning

Wave 6 broadened the family spread successfully.

Neither culture broke the rollout pattern.

Both fit the shared Mythic Clash grammar, both are understandable on sight, and both add useful matchup variety without introducing awkward new subsystems.
